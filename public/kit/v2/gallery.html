Punnett practice

Parent 1 has achondroplasia, Aa. Parent 2 does not, aa. A is the dominant FGFR3 variant.

What is the genotype of someone who is heterozygous for this gene?
Is achondroplasia caused by a dominant or a recessive allele?
For Aa × aa, what fraction of the offspring could have achondroplasia?
Two parents are both Aa. What fraction of their children could have achondroplasia?
Which genotype will never have achondroplasia?
A couple are both Aa. Explain why having one affected child does not change the chance for the next child.
