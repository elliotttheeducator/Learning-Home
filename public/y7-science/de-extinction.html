The return of the dire wolf

Read, then type
  • The dire wolf was a large prehistoric wolf. It went extinct about 10,000 years ago. Only its fossils are left.
  • In April 2025, scientists said they had brought it back. They changed the DNA of the grey wolf, its closest living relative.
  • A dog gave birth to the puppies. The scientists called this de-extinction.
  • Next on their list: the woolly mammoth, the dodo and the Tasmanian tiger.

Find the closest living relative

Game

How could we bring an animal back? Scientists follow four steps.

Study the extinct animal: its fossils, and DNA from its remains.
Compare its characteristics with animals alive today.
Find its closest living relative: the one that shares the most characteristics.
Change that relative's DNA to add the extinct animal's traits.

Match each extinct bird to its closest living relative. Think about shape, beak, legs and colour.

Woolly mammoth vs Asian elephant

Compare

Woolly mammoth

Height: about 3 to 3.5 m at the shoulder
Mass: about 4 to 6 tonnes
Body: thick shaggy fur, small ears, long curved tusks, a trunk
Home: cold grasslands (tundra) of Europe, Asia and North America
Status: extinct

Asian elephant

Height: about 2 to 3 m at the shoulder
Mass: about 3 to 5 tonnes
Body: very little hair, tusks (mostly the males), a trunk
Home: forests and grasslands of South and Southeast Asia
Status: alive, but endangered

With a partner, find at least 3 similarities and 3 differences.
