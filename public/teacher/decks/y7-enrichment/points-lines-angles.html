More words

Collinearpoints on one
Concurrent3 or more lines through
Parallelnever meet:  AB DC
Perpendicularmeet at :  AB ⊥ CD
Watch out. A protractor has two scales. Put its centre on the vertex, line one arm up with 0°, and read the scale that starts at 0 on that arm. A reflex angle is 360° minus the angle on the other side.

14An acute angle ∠AOB is equal to 60°. Why is it unnecessary to use a protractor to work out the size of the reflex angle ∠AOB?
The two angles make a revolution, so reflex ∠AOB = 360° − 60° = 300°.
