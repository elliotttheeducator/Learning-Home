Everything saves as you type. Press L to launch the lesson in the panel at the top. Resources are tagged Students (on the student calendar once the lesson is published), Teacher only (decks, answer keys, teacher versions) or To print (opens ready to print). To put files from any chat on the site, use Import pack (drop a zip or several files and each goes to its lesson), or drop one file into a lesson's Upload box; the Files button lists every upload, with new versions and history. Teacher notes, prep and anything not tagged Students never leave the teacher area.
