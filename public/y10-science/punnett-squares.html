Story first, 1 minute: Gregor Mendel, a monk, grew thousands of pea plants in the 1860s. Crossing tall and short gave only tall, yet short came back the next generation. Then press Next for each plant. Picture the two alleles as two voices: the dominant one shouts, the recessive one whispers. You only hear the whisper when nobody is shouting. Last press: the word roots and the trap (dominant does NOT mean common).

Pea plants: tall T is dominant over short t; purple flowers P beat white p. Guinea pigs: black fur B is dominant over white b. Draw each Punnett square in your book, then type your final answer here to check it. Start at Bronze.

1. Tt × tt: what percentage of the plants are tall?
2. PP × pp: what percentage are purple?
3. Pp × Pp: what percentage are white?
4. A homozygous black guinea pig is crossed with a white one. What percentage of the babies are black?
5. Two Bb guinea pigs have babies. What percentage are white?
6. Bb × bb: what is the genotype ratio?
7. A cross gives 50% tall and 50% short. What were the parents?
8. A cross gives only tall plants. Give two possible pairs of parents.
