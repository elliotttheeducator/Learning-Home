Title for today: Why do you look like your family?

5 min, silent then share. Take three answers. Sort them aloud into ones you're born with (eye colour, dimples, blood type) and ones that change (hair length, fitness, accent). That split is the whole unit: inherited vs acquired. Sort their answers with the pen on the right: students see your sorting live, and can replay it later.
