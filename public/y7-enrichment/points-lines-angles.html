Naming an angle

Two meet at the . Name the angle with three letters, the vertex in the :  ∠ABC or ∠.
Its size is written with a lower-case letter: a°.

Watch out. A protractor has two scales. Put its centre on the vertex, line one arm up with 0°, and read the scale that starts at 0 on that arm. A reflex angle is 360° minus the angle on the other side.

Example 1: Naming lines, rays, segments and angles

Worked example

Use the diagram to name:
a the line   b the ray   c the segment   d the angle marked with an arc.

Try it in your head first, then show one step at a time.

  1. aThe line
    line AB (or line BA): arrows at both ends
  2. bThe ray
    ray BC: it starts at B, so B comes first
  3. cThe segment
    segment CD (or DC): no arrows
  4. dThe angle
    ∠ABC (or ∠CBA): vertex B in the middle

Example 2: Classifying and measuring an angle

Worked example

The protractor has been placed on an angle.
a What type of angle is it?
b Which scale do you read, and what does it say?
c Find the size of the reflex angle on the other side.

Try it in your head first, then show one step at a time.

  1. aType
    obtuse: bigger than 90°, smaller than 180°
  2. bWhich scale, and the size
    The base arm points left, so read the scale with 0 on the left (the inner one): 130°
  3. cThe reflex angle
    360° − 130° = 230°
